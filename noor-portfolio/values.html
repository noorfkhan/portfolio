    values

    1. integrity is non-negotiable. do what's right even when no one is watching, and especially when it's inconvenient.
    2. objectivity is a discipline, not a default. check your assumptions before you check anyone else's.
    3. innovation lives at the intersection of different fields. the most authentic ideas come from connecting worlds that rarely talk to each other.
    4. listen longer than you speak. the best stories and the best arguments start with understanding the other side.
    5. lead by lifting others. a good leader measures success by how much the people around them grow.
    6. context changes everything. never strip a story of the people, history, and nuance that give it meaning.
    7. consistency builds trust. how you show up on ordinary days is who you really are.
    8. above all, voice the unvoiced. use every platform, every story, and every opportunity to amplify the people too often left out of the conversation.
